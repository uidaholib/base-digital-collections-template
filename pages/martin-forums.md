## Martin Forums

Since 1999, the Martin Institute has organized a series of lectures on international issues. Ideally suited to a non-partisan audience, these lectures feature experts from the University of Idaho community and beyond. Martin Forum speakers deliver a public lecture, visit classes when possible, and meet with campus and community audiences in informal settings. These have covered a wide range of issues, including war and peace, human rights, resources and development, and socioeconomics.
