366

I was worried about my stomach problems but took a couple of pieces, and it was a special Edo [2] taste. Seven or eight fellows from Po [3] city joined us. and a tea gathering unexpectedly took place. It was the first pleasant event. 

We passed the decision that the representatives from each region meet at the Hawaii headquarters for news reports from now on to share, dictate and report. I was elected to be the representative of the Northwest region. My duty of course is translation of various newspapers delivered here, and we started subscribing to local Oklahoma papers as well. 

My stomach problems are coming and going. There is nothing I can do about it as long as a proper diet is not available. It is another type of war.

Relocation issues

There was an unofficial announcement that we are all going to be sent to a different location on May 23rd. The destination remained unannounced as usual, and we were told it would take three days and nights to get there and advised to bring an overcoat for the nights on the train as the destination is in high altitude. As expected, various rumors have been rushing through the camp like a flooded river, and it is almost impossible to hold control over it. It is said that the departure is scheduled for next Saturday or Friday. 


[2] Old term for Tokyo

[3] Portland?
