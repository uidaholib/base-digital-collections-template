481

January 24, 1943 (Sunday)

It is supposed to be extremely cold, but we are in the southern region. It was 16 degrees early in the morning about a week ago. I have been showering in cold water right after I get up, but it was severely cold, and I was having a hard time as the bathroom is one block away. The temperature has been going up for the last several days, and now it is 80 degrees. It is still hard to believe it is 80 degrees in the middle of the cold season, and this is my first experience. I attended the haiku meeting at six in the afternoon. There are about 30 haiku poets in the club. I attended a meeting on Sunday after I was discharged from the hospital after a long absence, but I was having some stomach issues. I skipped the following meeting, but my symptoms were not unbearably bad this evening. 

Some fellow citizens held a baseball game on the ground in front of the fence this afternoon. Just a few were left in the building after a lot of us went to watch the game, and it was like being in a silent forest. It is called baseball but actually is softball, and it is not anything special.

Mr. Jinsuke Ueda (63 years old) passed away today after his long treatment in the hospital. He was a barber when I lived in Panama and detained while going through treatment after he fell ill. He was continuously hospitalized, transferred to the U.S. and became a patient in the hospital until today. I heard he was suffering from rheumatism and some heart disease. His doctor thought his death was a matter of time when I left the hospital.
