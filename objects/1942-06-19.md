384

June 19 (Friday)

I received an unexpected letter from the Japanese Assembly Center in North Portland where I was removed from. It is from Ms. Masako Onishi. She wrote about how her family feels after her father suddenly passed away and how she feels about the current social situation we are facing. She also asked how I am doing, and her beautiful handwriting is so clean and orderly as always, yet fully conveys emotions at the same time. I don't know how she found my address as I haven't written to her since I sent her a thank you note after she sent a bunch of snacks to me. Masako worked so hard for my newspaper publisher, and I alway appreciated her wisdom and expressed my respect to her. According to what she wrote, I was told by her father that evacuation [1] of the nisei generation would not happen while he was still alive. I can tell she went through a lot of difficulties to sell the hotel they owned. I will not forget about her kindness. 

(Mr. Mansaki [2] from Hawaii passed away with stroke)

(memorial service on the 20th)

[1] This is the word the author used

[2] Could be a different name
