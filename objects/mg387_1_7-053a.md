223

There was a fairly wide river going through the town. I asked a black waiter where we were, but he just gave me a very simple "I don't know" answer. There is no way employees working for a railroad company do not know the cities, towns and villages along the line. A strict "no answer" order must have been issued. 

A lot of people got food poisoned with dinner, and bathrooms were alway crowded. Everyone was furious and Rock Island R.R. was the target of our criticism. It was such a riot. It would have been almost like bombing an unarmed area and slaughtering civilians if someone had died from the food poisoning.
