March 5 (Sunday)

It is Easter Sunday when each church celebrates the resurrection of Christ with white lilies fragrance in the air. Someone like Mr. Osuka would have given me a pot of my favorite white lilies in a normal year. This year in the wartime though, no advertisements for lilies are seen in English newspapers in Po [1] City, and that reflects the sadness of the city. 


[1] Portland?
