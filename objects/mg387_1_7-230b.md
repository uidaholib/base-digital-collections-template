February 11 (Thursday)

It is Emperor Day, but our sixth unit is in charge of the mess hall. I have been feeling well since being discharged from the hospital but got a bit tired after working all day. My legs feel especially tired as I have never felt before. Laboring is not like playing golf, and even though it only requires light movements of your muscles, the fatigue you feel is like something you would feel when you are sick, not like refreshing feelings you get after exercising. I have not felt this for a while. 

Wing shifted blowing from the north last night, lowering the temperature and bringing extremely chilly weather. I woke up with the cold wind coming in through the cracks and had to turn on the stove and warm up. I looked at the clock, and it was 1:30 a.m. I went back to bed but got up at 4:30 a.m. I have a lot of bedding for me, but this is how badly these barracks are built. Our beds are especially poorly built, and layers of beddings on top do not help. We enjoy sunny days with spring breeze, and such chilly weather hits us all of a sudden. It is a strange place.
