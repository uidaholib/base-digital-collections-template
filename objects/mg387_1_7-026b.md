March 31 (Tuesday)

Forced Removal of Fellow Residents in Po [1] City

According to what is reported in the Po [2] City newspaper, construction has already been started by the United States Army Corps of Engineers in the State LiveStock Fair Ground several miles away from Vancouver Bridge outside of the city for the facilities to incarcerate fellow citizens living in the Portland region. The plan is to build dormitories for single people and single-family barracks for families by April 15th. It is reported that everything including meals are to be provided by the government. This is how the entire population of fellow citizens living in the U.S. will be incarcerated in designated locations for a duration of time as a wartime policy. 

Buddhist Memorial Service

A Buddhist memorial service for Mrs. Inosuke Takao was held at 2 p.m. in the 19th meeting room. It was attended by a lot of people, and the service was conducted solemnly and yet modestly.
