393

We could not help tearing with admiration and looking at him standing tall and walking straight. He is very well-built. We do not know how he ended up being a prisoner of war yet as various stories are told. We heard his submarine got beached on shore reef, one was killed and he was unconscious when he was captured. Another story goes that he landed on the shore and got captured by enemies while he was loudly reciting banzai cheering, but the truth is unknown. We will eventually find out how it happened, but we can easily imagine how the brave yet unfortunate soldier is feeling. I sincerely hope that Japanese in upper and lower status admire and praise their brave actions with both understanding and empathy.
