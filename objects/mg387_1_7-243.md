That means the U.S. government wants to hold rehearings for each internees. It will be favorable to present your donation history to the Red Cross American region and others. 

- It is an interesting phenomenon to witness delayed completion of the internee family center and the U.S. government constantly changing its policy. 

- Department of Justice views evacuation  and the definition of nisei in the centers as illegal, but the military executed the forced removal of Japanese Americans due to the unexpected turmoil. The current policy was created by the three parties, military, Department of Justice and the president. 

This school of the Catholic Church has been negotiating with the administration for the welfare of Japanese since the forced removal of fellow Japanese citizens in the west coast region took place. They had the headquarters in New York but moved it to Washington D.C. and are continuing  their negotiations. Of course there is no doubt that fierce protest made by the Japanese government was the key for the recent policy changes. Some heard that all the internees except for one percent would be released. 

The word "release" has always been with the internees, and even one day went by without hearing it since we were detained. A lot of the internees are having a hard time in making the final decision.
