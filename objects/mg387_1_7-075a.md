345

The wife of a Japanese teacher from Salt Lake Mr. Uchida, who is interned in this camp, is in the same place. According to a letter from her to her husband, she is staging in a bungalow, working as an educator with Mrs. Uchida (teacher at a Japanese school) at a facility like a kindergarten for children of people from Panama and working as a piano instructor as well. She said they are being treated well by the authorities, and the ladies are satisfied with it.
