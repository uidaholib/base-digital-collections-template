416

Their response is that it would not be possible to replace them with new ones now, and they will have engineers to fix them. It is currently being considered, and they will work to achieve what we requested. 

- Letters written in Japanese are all to be sent to the inspection center in Chicago by the federal government, and it takes a long time to deliver. Would it be possible to consider having Japanese inspectors in the facility in the future. Their response is that they recognize the delayed delivery of Japanese letters, but they cannot do anything about it. 

- As for leaking roofs on rainy days, their response is that management of the barracks is all assigned to the contractors. It is regrettable that the repairs they ordered have not been conducted yet, and they will push the contractors to work on it as soon as possible. 

- As for our request to live with our family in the designated areas who have been ordered to be removed from home, Their response is that it is not possible to do anything about it by the camp administration. Only thing they can tell us is that the federal government is currently considering that option. 

- As for dinner plates, the government understands a need for aluminum and will replace them with ceramic ones as requested. 

Our representative Taoka sent a letter and telegram to the Spanish ambassador to request a meeting with our representative. We questioned if the administration had delivered the correspondence. Their response is that all correspondence is to be forwarded to the federal government first, and they obviously believe the party has received it.
