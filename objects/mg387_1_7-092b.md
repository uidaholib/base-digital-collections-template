May 12th (Tuesday)

After breakfast, Mr. Kinzaburo Oshima from Hawaii (from Nagano prefecture, 52 years old) climbed over the gate on the south east corner, went through the first barbed wire fence, ran up on the second wire fence and attempted to escape. He was shot to death by a soldier. He was ordered to stop several times, heard the warnings but kept running at a faster pace.
