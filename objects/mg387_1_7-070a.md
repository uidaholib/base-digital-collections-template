340

I got a terrible headache the following day, had a fever and coughs, smoking was not enjoyable, and it was just like having a bad cold. I ended up having to stay in bed for two entire days from the day after. Dr. Tanaka gave me some aspirin as I told him that I had a headache, and it was all gone after I took one tablet. However, my stomach had a terrible reaction to it, and I was in excruciating pain and could not help screaming. 

I have been skipping breakfast, eating until I am half full and having just a few bites for dinner as my routine. 

I received a letter from Mrs. Kiso mailed out on April 6th. It is such a thoughtful letter to let me know that the newspaper publisher will be closed in the new future and I do not have anything to worry about. She also repeatedly advised me in her letter to directly contact Nakatani and demand him to send payments to me. He is collecting fees while I am away and pretending he had nothing to do with it despite advice from Mrs. Usuta and others, and that clearly reveals to me his sneaky personality to rob someone who is in crisis.

I received 25 dollars sent to me from the account of May, a daughter of Mr. Usuda. There is no doubt that this is from Nakatani.
