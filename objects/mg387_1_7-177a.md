447

Everything is rather interesting if we simply view them as what happens during a war, but drinking water in garbage cans does not meet the standard of a great nation. It is obvious that this simply ended up revealing that the administration officers have been negligent until now. I cannot help smirking when I see how the officers are hastily trying to cover up. 

I went to the hospital for an enema with the agreement given by Dr. Tanaka. I gave the treatment to myself, but I only had breakfast and not anything else. It did not work right away, and I lied in the hospital bed and waited for my muscles to tense up until noon. I talked about this and that with Mr. Okazaki, Mr. Ono and Mr. Tsuboi while I was there. It sounds like the hospital's condition is generally good, but I felt very uncomfortable when I saw soldiers with a gun walking around among the patients rooms. Mr. Okazaki complained about the poor quality of meals. It is not possible to stay in such a dismaying facility for ao long time unless you are seriously ill.
