387 

The submarine magically appears and disappears, and it conducts herself as if she had no enemies. We cannot tell how much bombing was done, but it is just the Japanese navy did what it needed to be done. At the same time, this could have not been done if not by our navy. 

There is no doubt that not only people from Astoria City but from Portland are quite upset. I also can imagine that fellow Japanese citizens who have their family incarcerated in the assembly centers are also shocked and largely worried. Therefore, it is such an inconsiderate act that some people reacted jokingly to this good news. 

Those people have been detained in different locations since the war started, and maybe because they cannot see what is happening in the outside world,  they do not understand that a war is going on.  They are just seeking comfort for their life and lacking the idea of nationality or ethnicity. They are full of hatred and curse thinking they were dragged to such a place and going through rough times although they did nothing wrong. All they do is to play with ignorant imaginations and to speak ill of others. They are not ready to accept hardship like a proud Japanese person. I am just stunned and do not have a word to say to those people when I see they are still asking for compensation for their loss and talking about it.

I received four letters in the afternoon. They are from Ms. Onishi, Mrs. Kiso and Ms. Ochiai. Ms. Onishi enclosed a 10-dollar money order and said it is for me to buy cigarettes or something else. I cannot help thinking about how I could repay all the kindness I have received so far.
