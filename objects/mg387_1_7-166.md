436

It is certain that they will push their unreasonable policies as their useful weapon on us if we give in now. This is something we should give our deep consideration, and it is a big issue. Everyone except for Mr. Sakimura agrees that we should respond to them with the most assertive mindset. I of course do not have any objections and completely agree with it. However, we are under an indescribably unfavorable situation and have been receiving unlawful treatment so far. It will be fine for me as I am going back to my county, but there might be more disadvantages and suffering posed upon those who are remaining depending on how the war pans out. I presented my opinion to Mr. Takeoka and told him that we should seek resolution for those threats issues through negotiations by the upper management sometime later. Everyone agreed. 

Mr. Takeoka and Mr. Sakimura keep emotional distance from each other. In fact, they had small arguments today. There is no way to find common ground for the hawky Takeoka and the pro-America Sakimura. Mr. Sakimura is condescending, and he does not get along with Takeoka who is rather slow. 

I heard that Representative Miyagawa accused Mr. Takeoka and said Takeoka is an agitator in English in front of the administration officers at the end of the meeting today. If this is true, I have to say it is Mr. Miyagawa who is an agitator. He cannot be trusted if he captures a warlord on his side and accuses him of being an agitator in front of the enemy. I believe he is the most dangerous figure and worth monitoring.
