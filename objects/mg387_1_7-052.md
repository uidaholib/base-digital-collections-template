222

Not only the quality of our meals suddenly went down after we left Denver, but also the service has been extremely bad. Tin plates are gone, and liquid sometimes seeps out on our laps through thin paper plates. Moreover, this steward calls us Charlie and has an insulting attitude. This is because our diner changed to Rock Island R.R. This is no comparison to  to N.P.R.R. 

About eight hours after we left Denver, an officer was kindly walking through the train and telling us to forward our watches by one hour. Also yesterday after breakfast, one officer was walking through with the steward and asking us a few times if we were satisfied with meals and had anything to complain about. This is probably a common practice for the army when they detain enemy aliens, but it is definitely a generous gesture by the military. 

I spotted a car from Kansas in a small town and also saw a parked car with a Nebraska license in another place. 

We passed a small town called Belleville at 6 p.m. I saw a green wheat field for a while and started to see trees here and there. 

We slowed down and passed through quite a few small towns in the evening. We were just passing the edges of the towns, but I saw a lot of black children playing. It was probably a school for black children. It also looked like a small stadium with a stand on the ground. I saw black people in every single house. It clearly seemed like a well populated city.
