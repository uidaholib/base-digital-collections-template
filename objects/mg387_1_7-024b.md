March 30 (Monday)

Radio news reported that all the Japanese living in the U.S. and Japanese Americans in the Portland City region will be temporarily incarcerated in the State Stock Show ground in the north edge of the city. It was reported that construction of the facility will start. 

We receive The Oregonian issued on March 28th, and it includes photos of a part of the Japan Town on the Fourth Avenue in Po [1] City. There is a photo of the front entrance of The Coast Times building with a padlock and some sort of notice still posted. 

(two pages of newspaper photo section including a photo of The Coast Times building in English newspaper) [2]
