At noon after lunch, the administration instructed us to report the name and the age of the dependent in the family incarcerated in a relocation center [1] as evacuees [2] within 30 minutes. Based on what was reported in a letter from a family member somewhere, an internment family center [3] has been constructed in Crystal City in southern Texas State. I imagine that the government is hastily trying to reunite the internees and the families in the new facility. 

There is rumor that dogs [4] are around in the camp. Moreover, I heard one of them is in our number 1906 unit and am feeling awfully threatened. However, those U.S. spies are not worth fearing at this point. It might even work for us to send out our messages to them.
