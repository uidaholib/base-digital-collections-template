401

Mr. Gen [Hajime/Moto/Tsukasa/Motoshi] Sata [Sada] [1] (from Nagasaki Prefecture)
The first one is our own Mr. Sata [Sada], who occupies the bed on the right in the space connected to the right side of the entrance. He is no different from when he was running a restaurant in Po City [2] before the war started. He was one of the group of people sent to Missoula later and staying in the 11th barrack, and he does not show any signs of sorrow or depression. With his babyface and well-built body, he is the healthiest of all in the barrack. Eloquent old philosopher. His vocabulary and speaking pace all reminds us of old students in the Meiji period. He also does not seem worried at all, physically active, and it looks as if he was on a vacation. He is the only reader in the barracks too and closely reads newspapers and magazines, and he seems enthusiastic about getting information about the current situation. He is not hung up with a lot of details, good at working with other people and is a likable person.

Mr. Wataru Takahashi. From Hiroshima Prefecture. Grocery store owner. 

Mr. Takahashi is the second youngest in the barracks but still is 40 years old this year. His wife and child [children] left in the assembly center in Po City [3] area still young as well, and he is another very cheerful person who never complains about the current situation. He is healthy and active at his young age. He is working hard for us as a mail delivery person for the 6th barrack. He is not sarcastic and very kind and naturally liked by everyone with his mild personality.


[1] Various name pronunciation options

[2] Portland?

[3] Portland?
