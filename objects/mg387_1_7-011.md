201

One option is for throughout a week. Letters are restricted to up to 24 lines, and we are not allowed to use both sides of the paper. Postcards are for up to seven lines of sentences, and it is not permitted to write on both sides. As for letters written in Japanese, the restriction is 24 cells in a column and 25 cells in a row. The mailing restrictions listed above could have some room for flexibility and rely on the common sense of senders. However, if everyone constantly mails out the maximum number of correspondence although not needed, and overwhelms the censorship department, stricter restrictions might be imposed. The name of the sender and receiver should be in full. They obviously need to be clearly written, and they request us print names to make them easy to read. 

My left cheek is slightly swollen due to the toothache. I received root cannel treatment from the doctor, but I am scheduled to have another surgery tomorrow.
