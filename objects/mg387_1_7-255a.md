He is suffering from high blood pressure and has some health issues, and maybe he developed a stronger desire to be with his family. It is obvious that he is nervous about news regarding being released these days. I think he and his family are sure to be united in the family center, but he might be anxious about what will happen with so much information flooding in about all the changes. He is religiously educated and has a high level of confidence. but he is mentally weakened due to his medical issues. I think he deserves our sympathy.  A rumor goes that shintoism priests are almost impossible to be released, and I have to imagine how he feels. I advised everyone against talking about release in front of Mr. Okazaki. 

Spring rain started to fall in the afternoon, moistening everything.
