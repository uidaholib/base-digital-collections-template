534

March 20 (Tuesday)

Medical check-ups were given to 75 people today. I heard that only a few out of the 75 people checked yesterday turned out to be healthy without any problems, and the rest have some sort of medical issues. Only seven out of the 75 checked today turned out to be healthy without any issues. I heard that they discovered that 80 percent of folks checked yesterday and today are mildly to severely diabetic. I received a letter from Mrs. Kiso, and I read that she and Yoji are both doing well.
