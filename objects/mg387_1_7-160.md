430

There will be a movie showing in the J Section  tonight. Many people in our K Section went out to see it, and I was able to sit out and enjoy the breezy and quiet summer night. It has been a while, and I would have to say this is one of the best things that happened to me recently. 

Some weird things are happening to some crews in the kitchen area these days, and each group ended up implementing some changes. There are sad-looking mess halls on both sides of the entrance barbed-wire gate in our K Section. The section is divided into two smaller sections K-2 and K-1 with each mess hall in the middle. Barrack residents in each section have meals in each mess hall in their section. Each mess hall has a chef, and we are taking turns working as cooks and others as always. However, it was found out tha ingredients in the K-1 section are constantly disappearing. Discussion was held, and people in the K-1 Section casted suspicious eyes that the above-mentioned activities were conducted by people in the K-2 Section. The truth, however, was totally different, and the K-2 Section mess hall security chief discovered that Mr. Watanuki (assistant co-leader) in the K-1 Section was eating and taking out single servings of food such as ham sandwiches and sushi. This situation simply could not avoid conflicts. The case was exposed by the other section, and we were forced to report it to the administration. As a result, each section in the K Section is completely separated, the supervising officers keep the keys to the mess halls and entering to mess halls is prohibited after work is done.  Mr. Kobayashi was appointed as the chef in the K--1 Section as the most fair and appropriate person. Mr. Watanuki recommended someone who would work for him, but the supervising officer swiftly rejected it.
