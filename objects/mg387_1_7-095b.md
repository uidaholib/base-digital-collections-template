May 15th (Friday)

I returned to the original mess hall for lunch today. They serve milk with three meals. The chief cook was very kind and took good care of us, but unless you are a doctor, my meals were just served without meat, and sugar was not added. It is the same for all the sick people and not really efficient diet treatment. Moreover, like the environment of the mess hall, the manner of the fellow patients at the table seems to worsen my symptoms. I definitely prefer my original mess hall. Mr. Kodo was the mess hall officer and made arrangements for me. This one is clean, and everyone moves quietly. Everyone here is my friend, and mealtime is my only pleasure here. It gives me such comfort. 

I was offered tea in the second tent where Mr. Horagami stays at night. Mr. Harada came to get me, and I ran there. Candies were also offered, and we also had marinated fish [1] that Mr. Harada's family sent him. It is a rare treat that we had for the first time.
