July 21 (Tuesday)

The Police Department has been recently established, and people from each barrack are appointed to take turns and serve. The duty is to collect trash like cigarette butts and paper pieces scattered all over the K-1 Section to make the area look nice. I was the one from the sixth init this morning, and this was my first time. It is such a silly duty, but it needs to be done as we are required to keep the facility clean. The task is easy, and it is important for us to try to save energy by moving efficiently.

However, the leader of our barrack Mr. Sakimura added some more work to this duty, raking leaves, cleaning dust on the roads and asking the others to help him.
