I am hoping for further Christianity education provided to him. 

It is widely said that Mr. Matsuura is almost losing his sanity over the future of his assets. He does not have anything to worry about as long as his family is living in Japan. The only issue for him is his assets in the U.S. His worry is totally different from the other fellows. Unlike the fellows mentioned above, Mr. Matsuura is simply depressed, and because of that, it is an apparent fact that he has a decreased ability to deal with other issues. 

Mr. Gen [2] Koyama is quite distressed. He explodes over tiny issues, and the personality is distanced far from who he normally is. 

As for Mr. Iketani, I can say he is a total pessimist. He does not have any other desires but to get out and go back as anyone else wishes. He was one of the loudest complainers, but it seems that he is regaining the calmness of his mind. However, I still witness his frustration burst up on occasions. He is the most depressed, and someone criticized him for being too wimpy. That is what everyone agrees with considering so many people are in a similar situation or even suffering from worse issues than he is. Mr. Yasui is also a Christian and one of the most important church members, but he does not have enough faith yet just like Mr. Yasui.
