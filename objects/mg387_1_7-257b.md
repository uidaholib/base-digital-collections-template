March 4 (Thursday)

According to what reportedly was told by visitors from the Manzanar Relocation Center [1], majority of nisei are refusing to voluntarily serve in the military, and that is getting some attention. Their reasoning is that, and there is no reason for them to serve now after their U.S. citizenship was ignored by the authorities. I heard about 7,400 volunteered to serve in Hawaii, surpassing the expected 1,500, but the number is quite low in the mainland. A total of 21 people have been ordered to be transferred to the family center 

[1] This is the term used by the author
