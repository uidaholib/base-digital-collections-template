511

I still have fatigue, and Dr. Takahashi from Hawaii (imperial university graduate) diagnosed that fluid is still remaining when Japanese doctors were sent to conduct check-ups in each barracks the other day. I was categorized as the "Class 3", who are not required to work, but I would like to contribute as much as I can.

Telegram came to let us know that Mr. Usuda's eldest daughter May had a wedding in Denver on the 11th, and he delivered the news to us. It is such a happy occasion to celebrate. I would have gone to the ceremony to offer banzai cheers in peaceful times, but there is nothing I could offer during this wartime. 

I went to the J Area and listened to a talk about Buddhism by master Okawa (Zen monk from Hawaii). It is about the Heart Sutra, and this is the fifth or sixth session this evening. He did not talk about the Sutra itself as our blackboard was taken away but gave us a lecture about the source of the sutra and the origin of southern and northern Buddhism. I am planning to read the sutra in advance before the next lecture.
