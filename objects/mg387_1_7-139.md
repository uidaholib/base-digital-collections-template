409

He is still inexperienced and likable. He has been isolated until he moved to this camp, joining the California group once and staying with the mid mountain group at other times, but he has finally settled down with the Oregon group. He was moving around for jobs mainly in the Seattle area and got arrested in Gaston, Oregon while working there upon the break of the war. I am sorry for him to live with old people like us as he is not even 40 years old. However, he is very calm in spite of his young age and never complains. I heard that he is in violation of the immigration laws and being here while forfeiting his bond. Therefore, Me. Nose cannot avoid going back to Japan due to a deportation order issued for him, waiting for an exchange boat.

Mr. Bankichi [Mankichi] [15] Sugimura, from Hiroshima Prefecture, barber

He is also one of the oldest but very energetic. He had his entire business taken over by his son and is relaxed and cheerful. He volunteers to render barber services while he is detained and works hard to cut hair for us as he is asked despite scorching heat. He is also one of the chattiest in the facility and the leader of dirty talk. Whether he is singing or talking, it is all about that. He is like an old youngster, and I find it interesting. I heard he is a drinker, and his biggest problem now is that we do not have any drinks. 


[15] At least two possible readings for the first name
