221

We know the train is going on the northern Pacific railroad but have no idea exactly where we are. Elder Mr. Sato, the chairperson of a Japanese association in Sacramento, has 10 simple train guides, but we still cannot figure out where we are. The guards are from Fort Lewis and also cannot figure it out although they are trying hard. We are sure that we are heading south from Billings in Montana and chugging along in the middle of Wyoming. We guess the next destination is Denver city.
