Naturally, complaints came up, and so did distrust and harsh criticism against him. People originally were saying let him do what he wants to do, and gradually started to say we should help him to a certain extent as he would like to show his loyalty to the military although he is pretty dumb. I absolutely stand against Mr. Sakimura's behavior. He is an advocate we have elected and merely a representative. He should be paying attention to the protection of our old body to promote a comfortable life for us. It is most ridiculous to sacrifice his own fellow citizens in order to accept the agenda imposed by the military. 

The barrack leaders held a meeting about the labor responsibility outside of the fence this afternoon, and they presented a report. The administration cannot require us to work outside of the fence, it has to be voluntarily conducted, and monetary payment has been approved even if it is on a voluntary basis. Questionnaires regarding willingness to work, as well as name, age and religion have already been filled out and submitted to the authority by the representatives. However, almost everyone rejected the recruitment of volunteers yesterday for labor outside of the fence. It is imaginable that the initial volunteers were hesitant as the details of the required labor were not available and also as due to the heat with a temperature of  degrees. The military found it highly disturbing and assembled the representatives today. One Lieutenant requested cooperation from us and went on saying they alway had a policy to meet with our requests. He threatened to eliminate the canteen stores, stop movie showings and prohibit sending telegrams if we do not cooperate and accepted their request for labor outside of the fence, hinting enforcement of the requirement from up above.
