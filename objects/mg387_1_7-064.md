234

April 16 (Thursday) to 28 

Our belongings were handed over to us today. Various rumors have been going around until today, including realistic ones like only basic daily items are to be handed over, and the rest of it will be kept and released based on individual needs. It is pleasant to have everything handed over without any problems. 

Each of us finally shaved, changed our clothes and felt relieved. We were almost embarrassingly animated when our luggage was pulled out one after another, and we were surprisingly talkative. Our thoughtless and frivolous selves were openly revealed, and it just reflects poorly on us. I got bumped into by a rude guy while carrying my belongings and tried to put my suitcase down. A man who happened to be standing and watching there suddenly sprung out for some unknown reason, hit his face with his thigh and broke his glasses. I think it would be impossible to have then repaired considering where we are. 

Our tent is the 7th one. We were choosing the leader as regulations require, and everyone recommended me to be the one. I am not yet in total health to fill the responsibilities. Practical Mr. Ono could not escape from being elected as the most fit to the position, and he was unanimously approved. 

Mr. Takeoka was appointed as the representative of our area. Mr. Takeoka reported on the meeting held by the representatives of each section and the supervising officers as follows.
