212

The country is facing its greatest historical crisis, and Japan is an enemy nation. President Roosevelt has already launched the famous "Quarantine Speech" in Chicago several years ago and has been repeatedly calling for denunciation against Japan. At the same time, he has imposed economic pressure on Japan and openly expressed his opposition. Moreover, he is proudly displaying its vast resources and the supreme power of the navy and army to the world and pressing intense fear and oppression over nations without means. He has been largely fortifying the military force in Hawaii and the Philippines and announcing to the entire world that he will attack Japan if the nation does not obey him. However, facing the war that started unexpectedly, his unpreparedness and confused reaction is clearly observed. 

He also made a large number of so-called, by many people, malicious arrests of numerous fellow Japanese living in the U.S. as if we were criminals. That is all based on superficial observation that we are a danger to national security. The detained are all U.S. citizens. As the war spans out, not only remaining Japanese living in the U.S., but Japanese Americans are forcefully removed from the designated area. Curfew law was imposed on all enemy aliens until the removal order was issued, but a different order was enforced upon Japanese Americans from any other public citizens. 

I am wondering if the U.S. government will deliver a fair judgement on the self-scarified Yasui lawsuit to challenge the constitutionality of the curfew. Observing the total interference with jurisdiction by the president although people talk about the judicial power, fairness seems hard to expect.  It is not only the biggest crisis of the U.S. in its history, but also grave wrongdoing by the military in the nation where political parties administer.
