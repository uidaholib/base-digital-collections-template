398 

July 3 (Friday)

It was cloudy all day, and it was comfortable without the horrible high temperature. Rules are set to take our beddings out to air them out under the sun every Friday, and they were reannounced several days ago. I do not like such housework activities more than I dislike bugs, but I took my beddings out first this morning and realized it was unfortunately cloudy. It is rather ironic that I was the only one who took the beddings out in the sixth unit. It naturally started to rain before sunset. 

I heard the kiosk is stocking beer and getting ready to sell it. I would rather have ice cream than beer. 

Supply distribution coupons are being printed, but it is going so slowly, and the administration is asking us to wait as it will take several days to have them ready. We are allowed to use two to ten dollars per week out of cash owned by each of us and held by the administration, and we were told to submit a withdrawal request.

I decided a few days ago to take a walk for about 20 minutes within the facility after taking a shower in the morning, and I am doing it every morning. I think it is about 2.44 miles of walking. It is helping my gastrointestinal issues, and I think my stomach ache is gradually decreasing. I do believe that such experience is essential to recover my health. I am also doing sit ups in my bed following advice given by Mr. Usuta. It is good exercise for the digestive system equally important to walking, and I have been continuously doing it. 

Departure for Japan is approaching, and mixed feelings are coming up inside of me, keeping me somewhat restless.
