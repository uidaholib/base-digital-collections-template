Following that, he was sent to this camp sitting in a seat throughout the long trip on the way. It was such misery that his suture finally ruptured during the trip, and he was moaning on his deathbed until he passed away. It is such a horrifying story to hear, and I cannot help feeling my pulse in my blood vessels. 

War is truly cruel and tragic. Modern wars seek victory no matter how. However, how would the world view the United States when the nation, which promotes democracy and civil rights, abuses civilians, especially so-called long-term resident second citizens, ignoring safety of human life despite grave medical problems.  

It is the very reason why we are extremely concerned about possibility of abuse toward women and children of the remaining families facing the evacuation [2] issues.
