August 6 (Thursday)

Cool air in the early mornings gives us a feeling of autumn approaching. Extreme heat in this region peaks in July, and it should be a lot more comfortable in a week or two. 

About 180 fellow citizens arrived at around 9 a.m. as announced in advance. Guessing game was going on initially, and we were saying they were from Wisconsin, New Mexico, Missoula and other places, but it turned out they all are our fellow citizens from Fort Missoula.
