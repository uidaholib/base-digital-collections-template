The thing that surprised me the most was that a troop of soldiers pointing a gun were following the lines from behind, squeezing them with a rope pulled on both sides. As I heard later, the supervising officer was worried and asked them if they wanted to take a break several times on the way, but they all refused it, kept marching for 4.8 miles and reached the new facility in the barbed wire fence. 

Unlike the one in Sill, this camp has semi-permanent barracks. The maximum capacity is 15 people per barrack, and the upper part of the barrack outside walls can be opened and closed from outside. All have metal mesh to keep flies from coming inside, and they have some glass windows as well for sunlight to come in. Air circulation is superb, and they are like perfect summer houses. 

There are three light fixtures hanging from the ceiling, and two stoves are installed. Each bed has a mattress, box spring, comforter and a pillow. Bedsheets are not provided perhaps due to a shortage of them. 

There are passes outside the barbed wire fence, and another round of barbed wire fence outside of the passes. Watchtower(s) have guard(s), but they are not intimidatingly armed with machine guns as they were in Sill. The camp consists of 10 sections, and each section has 34 barracks. However, each section is divided by passes, and we are forbidden to freely go back and forth among them. All the facilities are situated in the forest, and it is like an auto camp. If the food is decent, this can be a favorable place for good health.
