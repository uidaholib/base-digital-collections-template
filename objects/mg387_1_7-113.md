Barrack Mates

The barrack mates of our number 1906 Barrack (All of them are numbered with 1900, and this is called number six by the military, starting from one) are as follows:

Sada, Sugimura, Masao Nose (he used to live in Seattle but was detained while working in Gaston, Oregon), Okamoto, Okazaki, Ono, Sakimura, Sasaki (Hiroshi), Sasaki (shun), Shinome, Tanbara and Takahashi. As Mr. Sato went back to Japan, we are currently (as of June 20th) one person short and have 14 people all together. The seventh barrack has only 11 as each of them has his own likings. Friends would like to stay together, and some barracks have less people than the maximum capacity. People from Oregon State mainly from Portland and those who are from Wapato and Yakima and have been together since they were detailed in Missoula are occupying four barracks of the fourth, fifth, sixth and seventh.
 
Inspection of Correspondence

It is favorable that censor of letters is being conducted in a fairly speedy manner as assigned officers are directly inspecting them in a barrack designated for mail handling. Letters written in Japanese though are sent to the central government, and it takes extra time. It does not function as correspondence any more. Letters written in the native language are supposed to be delivered swiftly according to the Geneva Convention, but we are flustered as we face such times of stress. The U S. government does not seem to have capacity to take care of such details.
