He also talked about the philosophy of Buddhism among our other conversations about various things for about 20 minutes. It was very rewarding and satisfied my thirst for new knowledge. 

Secretary of the internees Mr. Gengoro Nakamura met with pastor Avely, a representative of Catholic "Maryknoll Fathers & Brothers", and details regarding us internees were reported by Mr. Takeoka today. Below is a summary.

- As for the internees family center to be constructed, about 30 units have been built, but the completion day is still unknown due to a shortage of furniture and other issues.

- The facility for families is like an apartment building, and each unit has three bedrooms and a kitchen. 

- New policy of the U.S. government plans to draft all the Japanese Americans incarcerated in relocation centers  at the drafting age and over and would like to have physically unqualified ones to work at farms and military facilities. They also would like to release issei who are physically fit and able to work to be employed outside of the camp. 

- They also would like to release internees who are redeemed not dangerous based on the observation for the last year into relocation centers and have them to be employed in some industries like the evacuees.
