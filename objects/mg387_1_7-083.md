353

Q: How are we to pay for newspapers and magazines?

A: Purchase amount will be debited from the cash hold by the military administration. 

Q: Would it be possible to make phone calls?

A: It will be permitted when it is approved to be absolutely necessary.

Q: Would the internees from Panama be allowed to visit the family?

A: Visiting is not permitted as the internees are not allowed to go out, but correspondence by letters is permitted.  

Q: Japanese English dictionaries, books, the Bible and Buddhist books are held by the authorities. (in Missoula and in Hawaii) Will they be returned?

A: We will negotiate for the return through the Ambassador of Spain.

Q: We heard that American internees in Japan are allowed to live with their family and are treated well. Is it true?

A: We heard that was what was happening in Japan as well, but we currently do not know how it goes due to the expansion of the war.

Q: We would like to ask the owner to be present when inspection of personal belongings is conducted.

A: We made our demand for it as missing items were reported, but such incidents should not happen in the future.

Q: Can we have occasional visitors to see us in the camp? 

A: We will ask for permission from the ambassador and try to meet your request if you request a visit.
