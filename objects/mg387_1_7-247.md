February 23 (Tuesday)

Below is what we heard trickling down from one prisoner of war. All the prisoners of war were navy officers, and they were unfortunately captured in the Solomon Islands, Kiska or the Midway area.

The person who told us the story was fighting in the Battle of Midway and was aboard on an aircraft carrier as an engineer. The iron board of the engine room started to melt in the middle of the battle, and iron fire balls were falling down on their heads from the ceiling. I was extremely dangerous. Intensity of the damage increased, and he broke the side wall open to the next room and went up to the deck as there was no way he was able to stay at the bottom of the ship. He did not see any fellow soldiers there, and the ship started to sink with a huge explosion. They all jumped into a lifeboat and drifted in the ocean without food or water for 17 days. They were not fully conscious when they were captured by the enemy. Several of them almost died, and they finally ended up being detained here as their luck ran out. The Japanese navy only deployed old ships to attack Pearl Harbor and conducted fierce attacks from the sky and the ocean. It was a death mission of expansive attacks with expectation that not all of the ships would return, but they returned without any damages. The result was better than expected.
