210

I have been trying to gain something out of this detained period during the war and believe that I am spending some time in reading, meditation and self reflection as much as possible. However, facing the transfer casted upon us this time, I am having an extremely difficult time controlling my anxiety. I am lacking calmness of mind day after day for the first time. I cannot even imagine the anxiety and fury that those who left their family behind are going through. We are strictly forbidden to share our destination, rounded up and removed. Should I call it misery or cruelty with our families also facing removal. I do not know how to describe it with my pen. 

The Christian church held a farewell service in the 19th barracks for the members this afternoon.
