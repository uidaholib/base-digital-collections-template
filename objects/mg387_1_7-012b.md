202

March 26 (Thursday)

Continuing to observe the members of the unit.

Mr. Kondo is forty years old this year, making him the youngest member of the unit. I heard that he left behind a young wife and child or children, and that he hasn’t heard anything about the current situation of his grocery store. Nevertheless, he never displays the sudden outbursts of anger that some of the men in their fifties do, nor does he shrink back timidly like a girl.
