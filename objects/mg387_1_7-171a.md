441

He should stay assertive and demand them to accept our request on behalf of us. He said we "had a pleasure to have lieutenant colonel Dunn" and the Consul General of Spain "is scheduled to come" during his report session today. His terms of respect are all mixed up, clearly revealing his pro-America attitude. It is extremely unpleasant and unacceptable. Someone like him wearing a Japanese mask should dye his hair brown and die. He ignores the Geneva Conventions while Mr. Takeoka is frequently presenting his strong opposition against the authorities based on the conventions. Mr. Sakimura even hints his opposition against the convention, but he of course has to as a pro-American figure. According to Mr. Shioki, the administration accepted various demands presented by the internees including himself, and it is because they negotiate without compromises based on the Geneva Conventions.
