March 2 (Thursday)

The highest temperature today reached 56 degrees, and the first warm weather was reported. It was in the thirties in the early morning, so it is quite unpredictable weather. 

Our elder Mr. Konoshita was hospitalized right after he arrived here and had been going through treatment, but he unexpectedly passed away at 9 p.m. yesterday. It was reported by Mr. Sakimura, who is his relative. The military police of the camp sent a telegram to his son when the elder was in a critical condition, and his son immediately came to visit. We heard he came back again to prepare for the removal of the family when the elder regained some stability.
