According to a report from the Panama people side, they received a notice from the Consul general of Spain right before the departed from Panama, and the summary of it is as follows.

The U.S. proposed to exchange diplomats and civilians living in Japan and arn soldiers in China, and diplomats and others living in the U.S. They are to be allowed to take valuables with them when they leave the country as well as one thousand yen. It was also proposed that their belongings not be inspected and no tariff is to be imposed when they leave. The Japanese government counter offered an additional request to include the diplomats in Canada and the central and south America and accepted the proposal. We were told that it is expected our exchange boats are to exchange people from both countries in a politically neutral territory somewhere at the south end of South Asia. 

There have been numerous rumors going around, and some are saying that the exchange boat is scheduled to stop at New Orleans, that those who are going back are to embark there, and the departure will take place on May 1st or 2nd. Any of them are hard to trust, but it seems to be the fact that the Consul general of Spain sent a notice and so on.
