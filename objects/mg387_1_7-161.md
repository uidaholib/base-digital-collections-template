Folks in the K-1 Section were not aware of what was going on, and their accusation ended up sterling up some unwanted trouble for themselves. 

Mr. Watanuki held ambition to become the leader and tried to promote himself to the supervising office behind the curtain while our leader Miyagawa suffered a food injury and was in the hospital for about a week. However, his action mentioned above was exposed, and also he had the ugliest argument about some issue after Mr. Miyagawa was discharged. He is marked as a troublemaker by the officer as a result, and all his requests were immediately rejected. It is also reported that the supervising officer clearly stated that they will not remove Mr. Miyagawa from the leader position, and Mr. Watanuki did not have any options but to express his resignation to save his face. After that, Mr. Miyagawa and Mr. Watanuki came to terms with Mr. Taoka as mediator. Mr. Watanuki started movement to have his resignation taken back and requested support from Mr. Takeoka through someone. He did not have momentum though. The administration viewed his resignation as reasonable and made a decision to accept it. 

True or not, they are only focusing on themselves, ignoring motives under the current situation or our future plans. They are even not interested in this enormous hardship where everyone in upper and lower classes pay sacrifice, offering their lives and belongings and doing all they can do for their homeland in this upheaval during the fierce war between Japan and the U.S. They are such pitiful fellows. 

There is a report that it is not permitted for those who are going back to Japan to take any new items with them.
