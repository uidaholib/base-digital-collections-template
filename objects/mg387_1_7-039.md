It was revealed that our destination is Fort Sill in Oklahoma in the evening. There is no word to describe our astonishment, and it became clear to us that we will be under military's watch. We are tossed around by fate and will end up going where it carries us. Hundreds of us from the homeland will be together tens of thousands of miles away in the middle of nowhere in the middle of this wartime crisis. This will be one of the biggest hardships for us, and we all need to prepare ourselves to face it. 

The kiosk ordered cheap suitcases and fabric bags, and there has been a line formed in front of it. They are extending their hours to meet our needs. 

I have been deeply anxious and depressed ever since I got arrested in December, and my health has been damaged by the sudden change of my life. I still have been restraining myself from directly revealing my feelings in statements or actions.
