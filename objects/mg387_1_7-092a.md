362

This Dr. Sekiyama is very arrogant, and I didn't like it. There was another person, probably from Panama, who was told to come as I did, and the doctor was telling him that he needs to do as the doctor tells him to do as the other doctors do not have any right to instruct the patients. He is such a despicable man to show off his authority over nothing. He is one of many doctors, and it is entertaining to watch his childish joy over mess hall assignments. 

The fellow assigned to the mess hall is a funny character. He is kind and takes good care of others. The way he talks is interesting, and it is as if he was feeding about ten sick people for free all by himself. It is not intentional, and he appears to enjoy taking care of people and is rather likable.  I feel particularly lonely when I am exposed to a lot of strangers, but I just have to live with it for a while.

I gave two jars of fukujinzuke pickles to a young man from Panama in the evening, and he was very glad, happily accepted them and asked my name. He looked so pleased that it made me feel happy.
