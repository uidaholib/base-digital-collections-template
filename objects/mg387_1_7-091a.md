"We Nikkei citizens are also pioneers and have been enjoying peaceful and comfortable lives with other people at our home Po [4] city with other citizens until this day. Upon the relocation order, it is our duty as loyal citizens to our homeland the United States to obey the order. We believe in victory in this war and should fulfill our duty as citizens. We are leaving this place temporarily but hope to reunite," and so on. It is a fairly large advertisement. 

Forced removal of Japanese living in the U.S. and nisei was completed last Tuesday, and the total number of the removed is 1,725. Families and single people of course were separated, and it is reported that they are scheduled to be resettled in the Black Canyon Irrigation Project (about 20 miles from Twin Falls) in Eden, Idaho in July.
