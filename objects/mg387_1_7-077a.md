347

According to the letters both Mr. Miyako and Mr. Iwata received from his family, rounding up was recently reconducted again in Po [1] city, and about 20 people including Kunihiko Tanaka, Yutaka Tanaka, Hiroshi Kato and Taniji Nakagawa were arrested. 

Mr. Takeoka is scheduled to report on the details about the meeting with the Consul General of Spain tomorrow. Our agreement is to make announcements such as mentioned above correctly at the same time by the representatives of each area, based on one recorded source. Some representatives though, trying to 
score a scoop, announced it this evening. 

Interestingly, in such a national crisis there is still this power struggle going on amongst the fellow Japanese in this camp. I find it rather comical and also sometimes indecent that they annoyingly argue about things about southern California and a smaller number of people from the north west and so on.  


[1] Portland?
