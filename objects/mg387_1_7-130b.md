July 4 (Saturday)

Today is the Fourth of July, the Independence Day of the U.S. According to the newspaper, the Independence Day synonymous fireworks are strictly forbidden, and not many speeches are to be made either. It is understandable when the defeat of the war is coming so close. We heard tens of gun salutes around noon, reminding us that we are in a military facility. 

It came to be a good time to introduce the facade of our sixth barrack mates. There are a total of 14 barrack mates after Mr. Sato went back to Japan, and the majority of them are different from the barrack mates in the 26th barrack in Missoula. I believe that I have some ideas about who the barrack mates really are through observing them while they are totally used to this restricted life within barbed wire. I encounter many occasions where the personalities of each person are revealed while living with them, some respectable some not, and it is naturally getting more interesting.
