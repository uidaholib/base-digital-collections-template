May 10th (Sunday)

It has been the full month since we left Missoula. Everyone is finally getting used to the meaningless and boring life here, and our complaints and worries are fading away while we are accepting it as our fate. 

A messenger came around noon and told me that Dr. Sekiyama would like to see me at the hospital immediately. When I went there, I was told they would like me to eat at the mess hall for the Hawaii region as they decided to serve all the patients there. That is fine. I don't want to eat separately from my friends, but it needs to be this way due to the cooking staff schedule.
