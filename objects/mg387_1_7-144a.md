It was so encouraging, and I felt as if I had seen the brave and tactful actions by the Japanese navy in front of me.  I heard about their war experience from the ones from Hawaii when I was in Fort Sill, but it was second-hand as they are all from other islands. The presentation tonight was done by first hand witnesses, and I felt jolted all over my body. Someone among them (Dr. Takahashi) was watching what was taking place on his veranda with a pair of binoculars from a bit after eight in the morning to around four in the afternoon. I believe it is easy to imagine what he was reporting. Squall hit us after the presentation, and it felt so refreshing.
