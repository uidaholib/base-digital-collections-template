However, he is one of the people who are expected to make a big leap to recover from the sufferings after the war, and I would like to see him further learning to gain more knowledge.

Asakichi Tanbara [Nihara] [Tanhara] [4] From Okayama Prefecture. Owner of a Japanese and Chinese restaurant. He once had health issues due to scorching heat at the beginning. He is extremely energetic but not  physically the healthiest. He is used to his carefree life, and it probably is also because of his old age. He is pretty chatty, but he is a businessman in his bones and never makes any offensive remarks to anyone. I have never heard him complain about the current situation. He is very cheerful. He works on his correspondence with his family members almost every day, with a dictionary and a notepad in front of his face. He seems largely interested in his future life in the States after the war, and I can see that he is having many thoughts about it. He is also a very thoughtful person. He is stable and does not worry about too many details, and I believe this personality contributes to his business success in Po City [5].  

(Mr. Shinonome)

He was my barrack mate in the 2th barrack in Missoula. It is said that he is one of the successful farmers in Wapato. He often seems to miss the point, yet he is talkative. As we left Hirosuke [6] Ito behind, there is unspoken consensus among us that he took over Ito's position, and he is the only chatty person in the Northwest group. He is also very loud, and it is sort of funny. I heard his wife and two daughters are waiting for him in his hometown.
