536

We might necessarily not eat it but miss it if it is not served. 

I received a letter from Ms. Masako Onishi, and it sounds like she found out that I was discharged from the hospital by my letter to Mrs. Kiso. She wrote that Hiromura asks how I am doing every time she sees him, but all she can say is she has not heard from me. She asked me to write to her if my physical conditions permit. I am planning to write to people I know after I get settled down in Santa Fe.
