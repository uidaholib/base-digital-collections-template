July 1 (Wednesday)

It has already been six months since the war started. The German military fought through the Egypt border in the Libya region and is moving forward to the military port of Alexandria. It is reported that they are almost capturing The Suez Canal. The port of Sevastopol has fallen in the middle east region, and the military led by Hitler is reported to be swiftly fighting its way toward the Kaukasos oil field. As for the Pacific Ocean, the Japanese military has full control over it and is gaining momentum. They have reached the U.S. mainland several times, and fears of the U.S. side are currently growing increasingly serious by minutes. It is observed that proactive strategies of the Axis powers will pan out on a large scale in the near future and will launch actions for the victory.
