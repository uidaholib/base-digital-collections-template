209

According to the report Mr. Takeoka brought to us at breakfast this morning, Japan and the U.S. are currently planning to exchange detained citizens from both countries with Spain as a mediator. Therefore, based on the demand of the Americans living in Japan, sending them back to their county by exchanging them with detained Japanese is considered. International merchants and illegal aliens are the target of the exchange on the side of Japanese living in the U.S., and strict investigation of the undocumented Japanese is conducted for the purpose. We were also told that permanent residents who have been living in the U.S. for a number of years could also be taken into government consideration if requested. Moreover, I heard the detention center in the east coast area has the highest number of undocumented Japanese. There are a fairly large number of detainees to be released, and the final decision was almost made but put on hold as the evacuation [1] issues suddenly arose. 

Nine cases of apples, oranges and grapefruits have been delivered to Mr. Hirano from the Japanese Grocers Association of Portland. They were distributed among the members, and we all share and appreciate their goodwill. 

According to The Daily Missoulian article on the Washington Journal [2] report this morning, the number and the expenses of Japanese, German and Italian citizens detained in each camp are reported at the congress.
