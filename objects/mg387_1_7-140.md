410 

They started to sell beer several days ago, but 3% alcohol is not strong enough to work for him. He must be one of those old men who would spend an entire day joking with a young woman if he bumps into one. He is truly liked by everyone in the facility and a funny guy like Mr. Sugimura.

There is no doubt that at least the ones in our 6th barrack ended up feeling calm after being moved around, getting completely used to the restricted life in barbed-wire fence, having our family forcefully removed from home and incarcerated and having everything going wrong all the way to the bottom. Danger past, god forgotten is the attitude. It is not only us in the 6th barrack who got calmed down somehow and are not upset. It is the current state for everyone throughout the camp. However, each of us should be fully prepared with understanding that the worst situation is still to come later. No one knows if the war will end by the end of this year or continue for a long duration depending on how it pans out in the future. We need to give our deep thoughts to figure out what would be the best strategies just in case all the internees are to be sent back to Japan. I am sorry to see the majority of the fellow Japanese do not wish to think about it with their ears covered. I cannot stand this meaningless life where we get used to the current situation and take it easy.
