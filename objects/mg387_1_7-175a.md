445

August 1 (Saturday)

It has already been a half year since the war started, and it is August. It is regrettable that everyone seems to be rather relaxed and observing the situation, without any connection to the outside world under detention. It is also due to lack of stimulation in the circumstances. Just eating and doing nothing increasingly promotes unpleasant laziness among us. We should be careful.
