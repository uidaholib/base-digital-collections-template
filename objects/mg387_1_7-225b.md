February 6 [Saturday]

I saw two elderly guys seemingly over age 60, one from California and the other from the mid mountain area, holding a discussion about one chocolate bar. The smaller old man saw a chocolate bar in one hand of the other and asked, "Is that a candy? Did you just buy it?" 

"No, someone just gave it to me." 

"What is it?" 

"Chocolate" 

"It is candy"

It went on. The one who was questioning was deeply interested and serious. It is just like a child finding his friend with a candy bar in his hand and feeling jealous. I found it both funny and pitiful. They are at the age to be grandparents living their lives without freedom in detention. I was hit by a sense of sadness and sorrow when I overheard their conversation. This paints a picture of how the internees are feeling, and it is hard to witness.
