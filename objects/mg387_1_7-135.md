405

Mr. Kosaku Sakaki, from Shiga Prefecture, farmer

Mr. Sasaki is a likable fellow in good mental and physical health still in his 40's. He is extremely quiet and ever frivolous. He could almost be viewed as sluggish. He speaks in a low voice even when he jokes, he is not bothered with anything, seems somewhat carefree and appears to be truly manly. I would imagine this is the type of guy diligent women would fall in love with. He recently remarried, and I have no doubt that he went through a number of family issues. He never talked about any of them though and us silently working. His attitude to know the limits and thoughtfully behave is respectable, compared to others who tend to get carried away. A lot of us do not recognize what is exactly happening in the current situation, are forced to work against our own will and complain but still try to please the boss. We need to be aware that we are currently detained unfairly by the enemy nation as enemy aliens. It is kind. He is another good one who would shoulder our future after the war, and I hope he is doing a lot of learning. 

Mr. Masaichi [10] Sakimura, from Kumamoto Prefecture, grocery business (Yakima)

Mr. Sakimura had been the chairperson and the vice chair for a total of 25 years and a prominent figure among the fellow residents in the region. He has been working hard to take care of this and that for us after being elected as the leader of our sixth barrack upon recommendation by a few members. 


[10] Could be Seiichi or others
