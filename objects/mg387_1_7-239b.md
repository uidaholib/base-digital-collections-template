February 16 (Tuesday)

The gift giving issue caused arguments last night, and voices against the gift giving issue and attacks against the committee started to be raised in each area. Attacks against the representatives were fierce in the mess hall this morning. 

Representative leader Nagamatsu visited each area today and told us that the plan to present a gift has been canceled. He was responsible for this and should have offered some explanation, but he was just delivering the announcement and showing no regrets. I don't think he is truly trustworthy. 

I heard former committee leader Mr. Miyagawa is provided with a monthly pay of  20 dollars by the military although he does not hold any official positions among the internees or work professionally.
