As for the Unified Combatant Command on the other hand, attacks by the second commands are called for mainly by the U.S., and it is reported that the U.S.military is planning larger troops deployed to the Iceland and Ireland region. I heard  the U.S. military also deployed troops to the frontline in Libya, but it is obvious that they suffered bitter losses along with the British military. 

If the U.S. manages to have all personal and military needs available as planned, this war will continue for a long duration of time almost endlessly, and at the same time, it will bring unrecoverably enormous damage to humans and the world. Not only is the area involved in the war larger than any others seen in war history, but this is one of the biggest wars with modern science technologies. As the war drags on , the nations fighting in the war will do whatever it takes to win. I imagine if we are not able to reach a ceasefire by this November, U.S. citizens need to brace themselves for a miserable life in the bottom of sorrows for the next decades. The carefree ones are detained fellow Japanese. They do not have any nationalistic views, are just concerned about themselves, doing nothing and daily complaining about their life. 

I heard Miyama somebody from Portland, a reporter from the Shinsekai Asahi newspaper in San Francisco, one person from the State of Florida and another from Cleveland are among the fellow Japanese who arrived yesterday.
