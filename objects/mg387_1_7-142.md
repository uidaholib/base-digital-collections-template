412

It has three steps, and you hear grinding first, followed by another to the other direction, finished with a long one in a different cord by one notch. This is repeated several times, becomes a continuous musical score and goes on all night. It is fairly loud as well. There is no way you would be able to fall asleep once you start to pay attention to it. Hirosuke [?] in the next bed snores. It is pretty loud as well but does not happen every night like the tooth grinding. The next one is Mr. Sakimura who talks in his sleep. This one is directed every night and produces quite a few pieces. If you have insomnia, you can enjoy listening to them all night. Unlike Shosuke, sleep talking of Mr. Sakimura is clear and no different from how he talks when he is awake with the Kumamoto dialect. We sometimes have a hard time understanding what he is talking about because of his dialect, but that is all. Interestingly, he always sounds like he is giving a speech. 

It is pretty noisy from midnight to early morning in the 6th barrack.

We were told that an U.S. officer will visit the camp for inspection next Monday and asked to tidy up everywhere. The administration conducted pre-inspection and instructed us that the empty space between the 5th and 6th barrack is not up to standard. I think we should just leave it as is, but some busybodies get busy and work hard to get praised. That is a pity.
