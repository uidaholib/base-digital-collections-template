Later on, someone (California) received a telegram from his family to tell him not to go back to Japan. It is also a fact that Mr. Takao in the 11th tent received a long-distance telephone call from the family of the husband of his daughter, and they asked him to send money right away if he decided to go back. Judging from those, we can imagine that exchange boat issues are largely reported in newspapers all over the U.S.

Not only radios but any newspapers or magazines are permitted in this camp, and we are completely unable to get information from the outside world. We are just counting days to keep up with a calendar and have no means to sort out what to believe. 

Moreover, the issue we need to pay the most attention and be very careful about is the definition of war prisoners. The basic meaning is war prisoner, but what are we prisoners for, and what is their purpose for them to keep us as war prisoners? It is common to be cautious about possible danger brought by enemy aliens, and it is also commonly done by a nation in a war, and approved by others, to incarcerate them in a preventional matter in concentration camps. However, it is of course hard to understand why this discriminatory method is taken against us by designating special areas, and it could be viewed that we are victimized by political reasons.

We got a shot again on the 21st. I started to have horrible chills in the evening, I felt terribly shaky and cold all over my body around 8 p.m. and grabbed my blanket and put it on top.
