He is not an excellent player but very enthusiastic. It is pretty impressive to see him motionlessly sitting in front of the game board with Mr. Uesugi in an evening shower. He sometimes talks about the miseries the war brings and thoughts about his life in the States after the war, but nothing sounds too serious, indicating his mild personality. I have no idea why such an old man is considered as dangerous and arrested by the F.B.I.

Mr. Shunji Sasaki, from Hiroshima, laundromat owner

He is truly mild-mannered and kind. He limps, and it seems to make everything difficult for him. He is considered as one of the elders and very agreeable. He is a totally honest person as you can tell from the fact that he has been serving as a treasurer for the Buddhist association in Po City [8]. He never takes off his shirts no matter how baking hot it gets. His philosophy against scorching heat is that once you take off your shirts, it is hard to get cooler. That makes you always be shirtless, and there will be nothing left for you to do against the heat. I am in total agreement with him and doing the same. He makes thongs for geta clogs, mends clothes and pressing garments for others. It is hard to watch him sweating so much, but he silently works indoors under 100 degrees to show us his sincere care for us. His son is currently serving the military, and the son paid a visit while we were in Fort Sill. I cannot help feeling sorry for him as the current living conditions must be harder for him than the rest of us. However, it is a blessing that he does not have to worry about his family just like Masayoshi [9].
