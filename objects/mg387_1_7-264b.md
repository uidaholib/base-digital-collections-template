March 24 (Wednesday)

It was announced that a part of the internees are to be transferred. 150 single people are to be sent to Santa Fe in New Mexico, and 39 people will be transferred to the family center. My name is on the single fellow list. Both Mr. Higashi [Azuma?] and Mr. Matsuura from our Portland group, Mr. Nakamichi (Wapato) in the fifth unit and elder Okamiya (Los Angeles, California) are in the single only group. 

The majority of the group bound to the family center is from Hawaii, and their families were just transferred and incarcerated in a hotel in Monterey in North Carolina last August. 

According to some information we received this evening from a trusted source, a decision was made to move the internees under military control, confirm the legality of our detention and had over the jurisdiction back to the immigration bureau under the Department of Justice.
