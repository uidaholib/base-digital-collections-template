March 2 (Tuesday)

False rumors are flying around like leaves from early in the morning. Life of internees is meaningless without rumors. It looks like we all are relaxed enough to say, "It is false" in the face of the informer and leave, and that is great. I went to the J Area mess hall to listen to a lecture by master Okawa at 7 p.m. It is pretty cold tonight.
