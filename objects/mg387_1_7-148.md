- As for our request to replace the letter paper with a certain chemical with "Internee of War" printed in front and provided by the government with personal stationary as we used to use, their response was they are not authorized to make the change as it is managed by the provost marshall, but they will negotiate. 

- That is about all, but I heard more details from Mr. Takaoka who attended the interview in the evening. I listed the details below.

- The inspection officer is Brigadier-General Culbertson, and he is an mild-mannered elderly general. He closely listened to requests from the Japan side and occasionally expressed his advice and opinions directly to the supervising office of the camp Colonel Dunn. 

- Dr. Kimura from Hawaii pointed out several health issues on food from his professional point of view. He emphasized shortage of milk for the elderly while the U.S. government encourages consumption of it. Their response is that milk supply in Louisiana State is almost non-existent, and there is some health risk involved in holding in containers even if they request large supply from other states.
