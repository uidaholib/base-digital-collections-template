I think the advice has some implications and is worth listening to. It could indicate a government policy to send all the internees and their families back to Japan, it can be interpreted that the public society would demand such a policy. It is highly interesting to see how the U.S. government will treat the internees after the war, and it seems that they are avoiding making any policy announcements of their future plans. However, Japanese residing in the U.S. have been a cancer for the nation for a number of years. Judging from how the government has treated us, I observe their intention to uproot, sweep up and annihilate us in the future.
