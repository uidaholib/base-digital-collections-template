February 16 (Tuesday)

The gift giving issue caused arguments last night, and voices against the gift giving issue and attacks against the committee started to be raised in each area. Attacks against the representatives were fierce in the mess hall this morning. 

Representative leader Nagamatsu visited each area today and told us that the plan to present a gift has been canceled. He was responsible for this and should have offered some explanation, but he was just delivering the announcement and showing no regrets. I don't think he is truly trustworthy. 

I heard former committee leader Mr. Miyagawa is provided with a monthly pay of  20 dollars by the military although he does not hold any official positions among the internees or work professionally.

Moreover, he is offered a payment of five dollars every time he works as an interpreter. It is also reported a position in the military with a monthly salary of 250 dollars is waiting for him after the war is over. It is said that he himself said this according to a trusted source. A lot of people think it is understandable that he is surrounded by fellows who are looking for opportunities to get close to him.

Dr. Tanaka is extremely unkind, and it is a nuisance as he has his favorites and unfavorites. Some formed an anti-Tanaka group, and are working hard behind the scene to convince him to give up his position as the hospital director and threatening they will hold a public meeting, launch a campaign against him and force him to resign. Mr. Horagami secretly told me that the group is recruiting members in the J and H areas. Mr. Horagami and I asked Mr. Tanaka to meet with us behind the barracks, told him about what is taking place and urged him to resign. Dr. Tanaka told us that he is ready to resign any time. I think it would be  best to let him voluntarily resign as soon as possible and avoid any damages inflicted on him. Stronger opposition will be voiced if he insists on holding the position, and it will not be comfortable for us from Portland. 

Dr. Sekiyana drew some fluid from my testicles twice after being discharged from the hospital, and they are smaller now. However, I need to see the doctor again as they are still significantly larger than usual, and it seems they still have some fluid accumulated in them. 


511

I still have fatigue, and Dr. Takahashi from Hawaii (imperial university graduate) diagnosed that fluid is still remaining when Japanese doctors were sent to conduct check-ups in each barracks the other day. I was categorized as the "Class 3", who are not required to work, but I would like to contribute as much as I can.

Telegram came to let us know that Mr. Usuda's eldest daughter May had a wedding in Denver on the 11th, and he delivered the news to us. It is such a happy occasion to celebrate. I would have gone to the ceremony to offer banzai cheers in peaceful times, but there is nothing I could offer during this wartime. 

I went to the J Area and listened to a talk about Buddhism by master Okawa (Zen monk from Hawaii). It is about the Heart Sutra, and this is the fifth or sixth session this evening. He did not talk about the Sutra itself as our blackboard was taken away but gave us a lecture about the source of the sutra and the origin of southern and northern Buddhism. I am planning to read the sutra in advance before the next lecture.
