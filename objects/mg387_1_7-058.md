Section II: Police and Sanitation 

1.	Beds will be made and tents and surrounding ground cleaned immediately after rising. Tent leader will be responsible for the cleanliness and sanitation for the tent. 

2.	Internees will all help in the cleaning of the entire area within the stockade. A general cleaning of the area will be made daily immediately after breakfast.
