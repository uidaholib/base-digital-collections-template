They held a hearing about the immigration officers and the interpreters at the Missoula Japanese internee camp and summoned Japanese internees as witnesses. They testified, and one Japanese and an interpreter were asked to show them exactly how he was punched. It was quite a thorough investigation. However, the Japanese internee could not really punch the interpreter there and refused to do it even though the investigator begged him to feel free to do it. As a result of various investigation conducted, the above mentioned two officers and the interpreters were fired, and the supervising officer Caria  was also removed from the position. The atmosphere in the camp has been greatly changed after that with more tolerant treatment for our fellow citizens, and the guards have a friendlier attitude toward the Japanese internees as a result.
