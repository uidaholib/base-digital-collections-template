July 2 (Thursday)

I heard Lieutenant Sakamaki has his meals at a mess hall within the fence and comes to the second mess hall for every meal. I was waiting for him this morning, carefully observing the outside pass to catch a glimpse of him. A bit before seven a.m., he gracefully came in with his face down and accompanied by one guard. I took a shortcut to go to the front of the second mess hall looking with Mr. Horakami and Mr. Higashino, but we did not see the guard by the fence gate. We waited for several minutes, then the gate guard came up from somewhere and opened the gate. The Lieutenant came in right away with the guard and disappeared into the mess hall. He is an average-height, masculine and young looking officer. He appears to be calm but looks pale. He cannot help feeling depressed with his unfortunate situation. I get teary eyed every time I see the officer. It is not hard to imagine how he has come to his determination of what to do after he goes back to Japan with his proud attitude.
