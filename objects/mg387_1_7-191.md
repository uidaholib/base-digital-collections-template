461

I am intending to alarm the government and authorities about such conditions and to call the general public for more attention.

The selection of missionaries and priests to be assigned to work overseas should not be all up to each religious organization. It needs to be revised how the government issues visas to unqualified monks who are not educated, do not have the right personality but just are able to recite a few pieces of Buddhist sutra. They just come to take advantage of a close-knitted community of their fellow citizens. It is absolutely out of the question to have them teach nisei students as their language school teachers. 

I believe that this started at the beginning of November, but the so-called Buddhist Association started to send a few representatives from each Buddhism school to pay a visit to the patients through each area of the camp. They do not do anything particular, and some just spend their time on silly talks, laughing and sitting by monk friend patients. I am pretty impressed though that they always bring a copy of Life and Pick magazine to the patient rooms. The Christian group also send representatives on Fridays, but they are always empty-handed and do not give us anything. 

I assume even Christian and Buddhist priests are totally discouraged and could not mentally function when they are internees. They are given the greatest opportunity to save their fellow internees utilizing their religious wisdom and rooted on their life as a religious disciple.
