516

He must be at the general level judging from his age, but I imagine he could also be a retired officer returning to serve at the war, or also could be the captain of the boat. They are all very tall, and the jacket distributed to them with big white letters "PW" (Prisoner of War) are very eye-catching. Mr. Miyagakwa is with them as an interpreter, but I heard they are able to freely speak English.
