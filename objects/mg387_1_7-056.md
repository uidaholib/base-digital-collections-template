Some managed to borrow razor from the Panama people from the homeland, but I see hairy faces everywhere I look. There is nothing we can do for change of clothes as they are not here. It is miserable. 

A set of Camps Rules and Regulations printouts was distributed to each tent. 

The heading reads Headquarters Enemy Alien Detention Camp, Fort Sill, Oklahoma, and it is issued on January 15, 1942.  The entire content is as follows.

Headquarters 

Enemy Alien Detention Camp

Fort Sill, Oklahoma

Camp Rules and Regulations

Section I: General Instructions

1.	It is the desire of this headquarters that internees be made as comfortable as possible. To this end, all available facilities will be utilized. Camp beautification by Internees is encouraged.
