118

It is Mr. Horagami [3] who did not get agitated after he gave up on it and gained a strong mentality. He got rid of, or rather lost in an awful situation, everything his success brought to him after struggling for many years. His suffering must be more than we can imagine, but he is relatively good at letting it go. He takes good care of the others as the representative of the barrack, keeping reckless actions by the others at bay and constantly controlling himself. I would respect that. 

Kadota [4], Kobayashi, Tomihiro, Okazaki, Otsuka, Nakajo, Akiyama, Fukuda and Ishibashi and Tsuzuki [5] are as quiet as woods. I heard of both Mr. Tomihiro and Otsuka's sufferings full of pain, but war itself is a destructive disaster that we cannot possibly judge by our common sense. They are fully aware of the unmeasurable viciousness of the war and made up their mind that there is nothing else for detained enemy aliens to do but to stay put. This mindset is impossible for those who are not able to deal with anything else but counting the age of the deceased child. 

The Matsushima brothers are to be the most sympathized. However, both are good at letting things go. As they constantly camouflage [their sufferings] with jokes and silly stories, and that makes my imagination deeper over the pains they are holding in their hearts. 

Almost all the fellows from Yakima and Wapato are respectable for his calmness. Almost all of them were engaged in agriculture or other related businesses and are probably in a more difficult situation than people from Po [6] City.
