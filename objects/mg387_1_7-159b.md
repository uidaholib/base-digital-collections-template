July 19 (Sunday)

Both Mr. Kobayashi and Mr. Sada are serving as a cook now, and they are worried about me and kind enough to do some extra work for me. I am very appreciative as I can eat nutritious food without dentures thanks to them. Some patients and doctors clearly show hostility against Dr. Tanaka. One patient is furious as Mr. Tanaka was extremely insulting to him and placed an order for canned milk to keep himself fed with nutrients. I am very sorry for that.

Mr. Kimura continues to serve as a mess officer at the mess hall. He fell on the slippery floor with lye water all over while cleaning the mess hall and hit his cheek bone next to his left eye with a corner of a table. His x-ray review shows he needs to rest for a while, and he got hospitalized right away and is taking a rest there now.
