206

Those superficial leaders who are not practicing self improvement and enlightenment on a daily basis would beg for comfort from Christ and Buddha now. However, it is not too late, and moreover, it should be most desirable that people freely search for his own belief from Buddha to Christ or from Christ to Buddha. I hurried away and escaped from the cross as Mr. Ban started to give his sermon. As our 25th barrack is close to the cross, the majority of people living in the barrack attended the service including those who are truly religious, those who are supposed to be a Christian and happened to attend to ask God for loving consolation for the current situation, and those who were just curious.  

I visited Mr. Fujioka in the 17th barrack who is sick this afternoon. He is making steady recovery and regaining his strength. 

Mr. .Hirayama with Konkokyo suddenly became an early riser several days ago. He had been the last to wake up in the morning and one of the least punctual. People badmouthed him saying Konkokyo is a religion that hibernates in the winter and starts to move in the spring. Reciting ritual prayer in the boiler room behind the barracks is a seasonal scenery in the spring.
