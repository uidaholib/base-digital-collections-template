375

The distance to the latrines, washing rooms and shower facilities depends on the location of the barrack, and it causes some inconvenience.

Originally, we were arranged based on alphabetical order and assigned in two different areas, but we obtained the agreement from the authority and managed to put the people from California together and also from the Northwest. All the people from Panama and Hawaii moved to the J area, and Section K is to be occupied by the others.

Each of us first settled down where we preferred to form a group of friends situated together when we arrived. It is shameful to see some disputes over sovereignty based on the preoccupation. 

Only one soldier with a baton is assigned to guard the barbed wire fence entrance for each section, and it seems extremely light. It might be because they finally understand how Japanese behave, or maybe they received a special instruction over how to treat us from the federal government due to an unusual situation we are put under. 

Two light poles are being installed once every few weeks outside the barbed wire fence, and more powerful searchlights are shining bright light to watch us at dark nights into a deep forest. 

Shortly after we arrived, we all were assembled, and the supervising officer of the camp gave us an official transfer acknowledgement.
