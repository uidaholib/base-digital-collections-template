200

The Daily Missoulian this morning reports that the Japanese government appointed former Minister of Foreign Affairs Matsuoka as the ambassador to the Vatican, and at the same time, Popo has chosen Cardinal Morrali as the representative to send to Tokyo. According to The Oregon Journal issued a few days ago, both Britain and the U. S. government expressed strong opposition against the ambassador exchange, and at the same time, American Catholic church associations also filed their protest. If this ambassador exchange between the two governments is true, we could imagine the call for peace could be a strong voice rising from a corner of the world and gain importance as the war pans out. 

I quickly read a letter from Mrs. Otsuka to her husband. She expressed her sorrow as she had her husband detained by the government and is to be forced to abandon her home she has been living in for 33 years and be removed to deep in the continent. It is full of sadness. It vividly conveys an image of suffering and tears of the family members of each facing forced removal. 

Report came from the censorship authority regarding mailing personal letters as follows:

a.	Letters in English -- 2 / Postcards in English -- 4

b.	Letters in English -- 2 / Postcards in Japanese -- 2

c.	Letters in English -- 2 / Postcards in English -- 1 / Postcards in Japanese -- 1

d.	Letters in English -- 1 / Letters in Japanese -- 1
