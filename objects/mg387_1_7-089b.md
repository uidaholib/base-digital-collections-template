May 9th (Saturday)

I had such a relief from my digestive systems problems this morning and felt great.

I had a small amount of rice with two pieces of funyu [1] and tea poured over for lunch. I hadn't had my favorite food for a while and was quietly worried about what would happen, but it was delicious. Naturally, I did not feel well after I ate it, but that was a crime I was willing to commit. Dr. Tanaka made an arrangement for me to sit at the table for the people with diabetes, but there is no special diet good for digestive system problems.


[1] フーニュー
