March 26 (Thursday)

I have my belongings inspected and sent away. Mr. Tanbara, Mr. Takahashi and Mr. Kiso took over all the packing and delivering for me despite the rain. 

Various rumors about the transfer flew all over the place as always, and it is rather funny. Rumors for internees are indispensable like rice for Japanese meals and bread for western style meals.
