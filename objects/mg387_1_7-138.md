408

He is surprisingly a big eater. His overeating makes any medical treatment extremely hard to be administered. He is in the hospital now, and I'm glad to hear he has been okay. He has been exhibiting extreme behavior and remarks possibly due to his fogginess in his head. He was selected to go back to Japan and applied to the administration. I would say he needs to continue to take care of himself and fix his head to return to his spiritual world.

Mr. Hisajiro [Jujuro] [13] Okamoto, from Okayama Prefecture, hotel industry

He was one of my barrack mates in the 25th barrack. He is healthy and hard working. He has already given up on his future life in the U.S. and decided to go back to Japan. He contacted his family in Po City [14], reached an agreement with them and is waiting for an exchange boat. He seems mild-mannered but gutsy and ambitious inside. I find him promising when I see him seeking for opportunities after he goes back to Japan. His two children are both still very young, but he definitely does not educate them in the U.S. I heard that his current wife is the third one. He is such an energetic fellow. He is liked by everyone and started yokyoku singing, English and painting lessons in Missoula. He is still eagerly taking singing and painting lessons by the instructors among the internees. 

Mr. Masao Nose, from Shiga Prefecture, no particular occupation

He is the youngest of all in the sixth barrack here. 


[13] At least two possible readings for the first name
