351

Demands toward improvement of treatment

Q: Are we prisoners of war or internees?

A: You are not prisoners of war but interned as civilian enemy aliens.  

Q: How would they be treated differently?

A: Prisoners of war are to absolutely obey military orders while internees are self managed. (The Japanese government adopted the United Nations regulations upon starting the war)

Q: How can the internees spend their money?

A: Military will hold all the cash and arrange purchases of needed items. There is a limit of amount used, and the maximum amount will be determined and announced shortly.

Q: Are basic daily life items to be provided?

A: Ten-cent tickets per day will be distributed to each.

Q: Is there any limit to telegrams or other correspondence?

A: One telegram per month to Japan is allowed, and domestic telegrams are allowed only when permitted. Correspondence is allowed in Japanese, but all are to be mailed through the authorities in Washington D.C. 

Q: How can we purchase and send belongings?

A: You can purchase items but cannot send them.

Q: Would it be possible to get back the assets held in the continent, Hawaii and Panama?

A: We will try to make it happen by negotiating through the Consul General of Spain in Washington D.C.
