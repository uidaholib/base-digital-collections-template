April 12 (Sunday)

The train arrived at a large city at 4 a.m. We found out we are in Denver. 
Our train needs to arrive early in the morning. It was almost pitch dark and totally silent in the station. This is what we found out later, but a young man named something Yamane was sent over to join us aboard after being detained at a county jail in Salt Lake city for about four months. 

The train departed at 5:20 a.m. after staying at the station for about one hour and 20 minutes. We were again going on through the dark. There seemed to be oil fields out there, and I saw oil rigs. They might be for test drilling. What I noticed was that I did not see cars on highways. Not seeing any cars on highways in this nation of automobiles tells us how things go in wartime. The result caused by control over oil and rubber consumption is apparent.
