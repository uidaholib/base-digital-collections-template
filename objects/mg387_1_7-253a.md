523

A party to celebrate wedding for the daughter of Mr. Mitsusaku Nishino was held at the K-1 mess hall at 6:30 in the afternoon. His daughter will hold their wedding ceremony at a Methodist Church in Twin Falls in Idaho State. About 70 people attended, and it was a joyous gathering with some performances played. It is an unusual wedding party for the internees, but under these circumstances, there is no way we can be happy from the bottom of our hearts.

Three new internees were detained the day before yesterday. I heard that one of them was transferred from Galveston City in Texas.
