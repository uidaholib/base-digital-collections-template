June 20 (Saturday)

I received a reply letter from Ms. Ogo. I sent a letter to Ms. Nobuko Ochiai, and next to Ms. Ogo, and that is the reply from her. I left a black briefcase with Japanese pajamas and a few other items in it at Foster Hotel. I asked the two to talk to each other to ask Mr. Johnson, a clerk at the hotel to send it to me and also to pay the postage in advance for me if possible.
