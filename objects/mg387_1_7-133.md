He is one of the people waiting to be on an exchange boat, and he is another one who does not seem worried and appears pretty comfortable. He slams doors every time he opens one as if no one was around him. I think it should be forgiven, along with his loud voice, as he has formed this habit while living on a farm for such a long time with neighbors' houses far away from his. He is in good health and physically active in spite of the heat, as his body has been trained with farming for so many years. You can tell that he is what he looks like without any malicious thoughts inside. He is likable. He is from Ibaraki Prefecture. 

Mr. Masayoshi Sasaki, from Hiroshima, hotel owner

He is 60 years old and the oldest of us. He is small but extremely fit. He is well-known as a Buddhist, and rather known as a fanatic among all the fellow Po City [7] people. We hear him repeatedly chanting Buddhist mantras in the bed when he wakes up. He is a simple old man and behaves and speaks quietly. I am sorry for him as he is always asked to help and is working hard. When we were making a silly garden, all the hands were required to participate in the project in scorching heat. He hesitated first when he was asked but went out and worked hard with sweat pouring down. I could not help  My anger fuming when he later talked about it and said, "It was very hard". I have decided to be totally respectful and protective to him ever since. He is a Japanese chess champion in the camp.
