411

I am certain that their biggest goal is to reunite with their family, but they will need to move forward for their future right away. I believe that it is most important to have well thought out plans and be prepared, and at the same time to intelligently work on the planning. We should not only be interested in the current daily war reporting but also should understand what our future will be like as an alien resident, pay attention to  the political and social negotiations made by various agencies and departments and be prepared to revive in the upcoming new era. My fellow Japanese seem to be too ignorant and easy going to do that. 

Everyone in the 6th barrack is mild-mannered, and we are considered as the most quiet among the people in the Northwest barracks. We just do not make a racket or make loud noises after hours. The noisiest is elder Sugimura followed by Sada, Takahashi, Shinonome and Tanbara [Nihara][Nibara] [16] in this order. They monopolize the stage during the day, but it is like silence after a storm after bedtime. 

It is fully that elder Shosuke [?] Sasaki, who is the quietest and mildest of all, Shunji, Hirosuke [?] and Mr. Sakimura in order of their beds makes noises and movements in dark nights with each sound like a brass instrument orchestra. First of all, Shosuke talkies fast in his sleep about something we do not understand to start with. Shunji loudly grinds his teeth. His tooth grinding is the most strange thing in the world, compatible with the snoring of Hirayama, the Konkokyo believer.
