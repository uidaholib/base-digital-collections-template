438

I received a letter from Ms. Masako Onishi.

Newspaper articles do not report any update on the war. The War Information Office was newly established, and that has made all the media spineless. I don't even feel like reading newspapers and pay serious attention to the articles anymore. 

It turns out that 20 people responded they would not like to go back to Japan on the questionnaire, adding three people who changed their mind to go back to the homeland. It is estimated that the second exchange boat will depart on August 10th or 20th.
