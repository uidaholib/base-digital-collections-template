380

Inspired by his garden, the other barracks started building flower gardens one after another. They also are making vegetable beds here and there and enjoying the produce. 

However, Mrl Sakimura volunteers to renovate and expand the round garden designed by Mr. Ono, started to build hexagon flower beds on the right and left sides and made a big deal deploying everyone in the barrack. We old folks were sweating to carry logs and pile dirt in scorching heat, and it was nothing but a waste of energy for us. I was just stunned to see what we ended up doing was planting some weed, pulling a wimpy young pine tree, carrying it over and planting it in the middle of it. Also, this pine tree is about 10 feet and has a few awkward-looking bundles of pine needles on the top just like messed up hair of a prostitute who just woke up. Big and small pine trees are all around. Native pine trees are offering nature decoration on both sides of the barrack. I have to say that bringing and proudly planting another pine tree in this environment is merely a tasteless and aimless action. It is like being offered piping hot sweet bean soup on an extremely hot afternoon. It is such a redundant idea like putting a roof over a roof. No consideration for natural beauty and no understanding of gracefulness. It is nothing more than assembling stuff to shape a form to satisfy materialistic desires. I despise that. 

Extreme Heat

The heat is unbearable. It is not unusual to have the temperature at 95 or 96 degrees in the barracks on sunny days. I used to get dizzy at first and wanted to lay down on the ground many times.
