204

He should openly smoke with everyone else. It should be publicly accepted. 

Mr. Hirayama with the Konkokyo church takes care of things around himself first, and on occasions, it seems that he ignores others and acts like there was nobody but himself. However, that is not because he is selfish. He is simply clueless and not sensitive enough to notice what is going on. It is not intentional, and if you give him a piece of advice, he will deeply reflect on that and try to improve himself. He is rather likable. However, although he is a preacher continuously devoting his life to the religion, his philosophy seems to simply control and observe himself, and he does not take any actions to save others at all. Religion has no place in this camp. I have to say it is unfortunate that the public views religious mission as a means to make a living.　However, it is fair to say that he is a friendly person. He is one of the leader figures in the barrack and does not express his dissatisfaction with the current situation. 

Missionary Terakawa and another person received parole and left the camp. The first is to live in Salt Lake City and the latter in Ogden. 

Mrs. Tanaka, Mrs. Ishibashi and her two daughters came to visit from Po [8] City. I heard each of them said the families in Po [9]City are feeling very anxious and having a hard time deciding what actions to take regarding the evacuation [10].
