May 5 (Tuesday)

At 10 a.m. today, members of our area (the fourth) gathered at the mess hall to be fully reported on the meeting with the Consul General of Spain held yesterday. The meeting minutes of the meeting was read, and Mr. Mukada had a question and answer segment to provide more detailed information. The details are as follows.

Meeting minutes of Consul General of Spain in New Orleans Gale [1], Mr. Young of the Department of State and Major Eckel held on May 4, 1942.
