399 131

Mr. Takaoka said that It is a shame to be detained and aimlessly spending everyday. He thinks we should record all the details that have happened to us since we were sent to Missoula, wait for a peaceful time to come back to put them together and commemorate the records as a piece of history outside of the mainstream war record for the future. For example, we have numerous situations such as the meeting with the Consul General of Spain and negotiations with the military administration. However, representatives we elected sometimes do not show approval of such ideas of ours, and I don't understand how they truly feel about it. He thinks the people from the Northwest, or Oregon State, should act independently and come up with some plan, and he shared his ideas and asked for my opinions.  I am absolutely in agreement with him. We should come up with plans and start working on it right away. Both of us agreed to hold a meeting with Oregon people and others soon. 

I don't know who caught it, but someone showed me a beautiful snake in the bottle this morning. According to a soldier, it is called coral snake and one of the most dangerous poisonous snakes. It is said that once you get bitten, you would lose your life within about six minutes. I thought all snakes were scary looking, but as the name indicates, the coral snake is truly coral colored adorned with black rings, just like coral decorations Japanese ladies tied to their hair a long time ago.
