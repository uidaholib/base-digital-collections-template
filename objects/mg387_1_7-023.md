213

As long as the nation is facing the current situation, unreasonable means can be forcefully taken. Moreover, many people ignore the constitutions regarding the treatment of Japanese Americans mixed with the enemy nation Japan, and it is undoubted that a certain number of the anti-Japanese will take advantage of the current climate in effort of defeating them. It is also unmistakable that the public will go with the flow and call for actions. 

I think the result of such a test case by nisei now would create an intense situation for the future. It is extremely interesting and at the same time makes me unbearably anxious. 

Tonight, according to Mr. Takeshi [1] Nakazawa from Los Angeles, extremely inhumane and vicious treatment conducted in the California region was exposed while arresting our fellow Japanese living in the U.S. Mr. Seki, who passed away after he was detained in this camp, was told by his doctor that he had two months left to go after a major surgery. However, he was arrested, detained in a federal government prison, and temporarily put in a hospital unit. They took turns and delivered meals to his hospital room, but when it rained, a tray carrying his meal was constantly rained down on in a block and a half distance in between. Bread got completely soaked and was unable to be consumed, and although he requested military police officers a lid to be placed over, it was totally denied and not granted. This seriously sick person was forced to be fed with food soaked with rainwater. Later on, this patient went aboard a train with the other people at 1 in the afternoon, but the train did not depart, and he was forced to sit in the train with all the windows closed until 9 in the evening. 


[1] Ken? Takashi?
