May 7th (Wednesday)

Report regarding mailing was announced this afternoon. Letters can be written on both sides of paper with a 1.2-inch margin up to 2,000 characters. Write "Internee's Mail" on the upper left corner of the envelope and "Free" in the section for stamps. Write the sender's full name, add the internee's special number, and the camp number below that.
