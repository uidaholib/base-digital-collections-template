495

If Mr. and Mrs. Chiang comes to the U.S., it will be viewed as fleeing due to internal uprising of rebellion. History of China is the repetition of escaping from dangers nearby. Collapse of the Chiang administration will surely bring a peace treaty between Japan and China, and that will trigger a possible big leap in Japanese diplomatic policies toward the Soviet Union. It will call for ratification of the treaty between Germany and the Soviet Union, and Japan will attack the Soviet Union with a blow from behind if they refuse to accept it. This step should be in Japan's future war strategies against the U.S.
