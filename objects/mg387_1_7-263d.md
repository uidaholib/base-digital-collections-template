March 19 (Friday)

Medical check-ups were conducted in a ward of the interment hospital at 2 p.m. All the physicians are military doctors, and it was for the first group, and 25 people who have no medical issues from the fourth, fifth and sixth units among the Oregon group went to the hospital. The doctors saw all of us one after another, and it started with measuring the height followed by a fairly detailed check-up. A rumor goes that the administration hastily scheduled this check-up session upon receiving the internees are in poor health condition, but the truth is unknown.
