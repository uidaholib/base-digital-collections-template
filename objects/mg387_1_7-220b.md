February 2 (Tuesday)

One of the guard soldiers came to us, being drunk. I heard he starts drinking in the morning and keeps drinking every day. This is something you can encounter only in U.S. military camps. 

Newspaper reported that the Japanese government announced an ocean battle in the Solomon Island area (Ramel Island) is taking place, and they sank two battleships and three cruisers. They also damaged another battleship and another cruiser in the following morning. People in the camp were extremely joyous. Someone said this is the month of the Empire Day, and good news will come pouring in from now on. Mr. Okasaki told us that it is 1943 [Showa 18] this year. People always say even ogres or bad tea are good at age 18. This will be the first year for the Japanese military to accelerate for huge leaps. He was a soldier and is still full of fighting spirit.
